# Character + Scripture synergy

Bible Quest's differentiator is not Card → Effect but:

```text
Scripture Card → Which character? → Which target? → Which timing? → What consequence?
```

## Patterns from mature games (adapt, don't copy)
- **Card played *through* a unit** — the unit determines how the card resolves (Pokémon attachments,
  equipment-style targeting).
- **Affinity** — a card has a base effect for anyone and a stronger/different effect with a matching
  character (Runeterra region/champion identity).
- **Character-locked cards** — only one character can use it; strong identity, more dead-card risk.
- **Champion progression inside a battle** — a character changes after meeting a condition
  (Runeterra level-up). David's Faith → Against the Giant → Sling Stone already follows this shape.

## How the three characters could differ (design space — not a to-do list)
| Character | Story identity | Natural Scripture affinity |
|---|---|---|
| David | Shepherd who trusts God against the giant | Faith, courage-in-battle, the 1 Samuel 17 declarations; turns Faith into damage |
| Samuel | Prophet who hears and anoints | Prayer, anointing, removing Fear, building David's Faith |
| Jonathan | Covenant friend who protects | Shield, covenant, encouragement, Courage |

## Rules of thumb
- Prefer **one shared hand with character targeting** over per-character decks in a 3-character prototype.
- Keep the base effect useful for anyone so cards are rarely dead; put identity in the *bonus* or *shape*.
- A synergy must be **visible before playing** (highlight the best character, show the bonus).
- Every synergy should be explainable as story: "Samuel anoints", "Jonathan's covenant", "David's faith".
- Do not add synergies, new cards or characters without the user's approval; propose them with numbers.
